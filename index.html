About Us

Izak English Medium School and Junior College has a proud history of providing quality education for over 20 years. Established by the Alfa Social and Educational Foundation, our institution focuses on holistic development, strong academic foundations, and building confident future leaders.

We follow the State Board curriculum and offer modern facilities including science labs, computer education from Grade 1, a library with thousands of books, and robust sports and extracurricular programs.
