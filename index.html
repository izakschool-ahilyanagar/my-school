A philosophy built
on plain sight.

We don't measure growth by test season. We measure it the way you'd read a cross-section of a trunk — season by season, out loud, in front of the whole community.

01

Taught, not tracked

Small advisory groups follow a student across subjects, so no single teacher is guessing at the whole picture.

02

Made, not memorized

Every discipline ends in a made thing — a built bridge, a staged scene, a published paper — not just a score.

03

Slow, on purpose

We resist the rush to "cover material." Depth in fewer places outlasts breadth in many.

How to join
the trunk.

Admissions runs on a rolling basis for mid-year openings, with a primary round each fall for the following school year.

Step 1

Visit

Tour the campus on a regular school day — no staged assemblies.

Step 2

Apply

Submit records and a short written reflection — from the student, not a parent.

Step 3

Spend a Day

Applicants shadow a class in their grade band for a full day.

Step 4

Decide

Offers go out on a rolling basis, typically within three weeks.

Start an inquiry

Tell us a little about your student and we'll follow up to schedule a visit within two business days.
