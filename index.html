About Izak

Established to provide quality education to students from diverse backgrounds, Izak English Medium School and Junior College focuses on holistic development — academics, sports, arts and values. We offer Nursery through Grade 12 on the State Board syllabus, computer education from Grade 1, science labs, and a resource-rich library.

  • Experienced & qualified teaching staff
  • Computer lab and smart classrooms
  • Physics, Chemistry & Biology labs
  • Library with 5000+ books
  • School bus within 10 km
  • Hostel options nearby
